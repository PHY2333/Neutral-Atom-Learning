$$
	\braket{ j_{1}m_{1},j_{2}m_{2} | JM }=(-1)^{j_{1}+j_{2}-J}\braket{ j_{2}m_{2},j_{1}m_{1} | JM } 
$$
写成简化形式即:
$$
	C_{j_{2}m_{2},j_{1}m_{1}}^{JM}=(-1)^{j_{1}+j_{2}-J}C_{j_{1}m_{1},j_{2}m_{2}}^{JM}
$$
## 例子：[[Hund规则]]
对于碳原子的$2p$电子组态，$l=1$
$$
	C_{lm_{2},lm_{1}}^{LM}=(-1)^{2l-L}C_{lm_{1},lm_{2}}^{LM}
$$
又
$$
	\ket{(ll)LM} = \sum \limits_{m_{1}m_{2}}C_{lm_{1},lm_{2}}^{LM}\ket{lm_{1}} \ket{lm_{2}}
$$
所以$L=1$时空间波函数反对称
