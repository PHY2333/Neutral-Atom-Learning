一个偶极子电势$\phi(\mathbf{r})$为：
$$
	\phi(\mathbf{r})=\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}} \frac{\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{r}}{r^{3}}
$$
电场$\mathbf{E}_{1}(\mathbf{r})$为：
$$
	\mathbf{E}_{1}(\mathbf{r})=-\nabla \phi(\mathbf{r})=-\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\nabla\left( \frac{\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{r}}{r^{3}} \right)
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
	\nabla \left( \frac{\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{r}}{r^{3}} \right)&=\frac{1}{r^{3}}\nabla(\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{r})+(\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{r})\nabla \left( \frac{1}{r^{3}} \right)	\\
	&=\frac{\mathbf{p}_{1}}{r^{3}}-\frac{3(\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{r})\mathbf{r}}{r^{5}}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
	\mathbf{E}_{1}(\mathbf{r})=\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\left[ \frac{3(\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \hat{\mathbf{r}})\hat{\mathbf{r}}-\mathbf{p}_{1}}{r^{3}} \right]
$$
电偶极子在电场中的势能$U$：
$$
	U=-\mathbf{p}_{2}\cdot \mathbf{E}_{1}(\mathbf{r})
$$
$$
	U=\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}} \frac{\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \mathbf{p}_{2}-3(\mathbf{p}_{1}\cdot \hat{\mathbf{r}})(\mathbf{p}_{2}\cdot \hat{\mathbf{r}})}{r^{3}}
$$
